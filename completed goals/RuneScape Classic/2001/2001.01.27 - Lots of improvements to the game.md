## Goals

### Skill

- [x] 10 Prayer - *Rock skin*
  
*Rock skin as a spell was released here. The modern equivalent to this is Rock skin (Prayer) which requires 10 prayer to use.*

### Item

- [x] (*Pushed*) Obtain a diamond - *On the 23. January 2001, a diamond was put in the vault. It was not possible to obtain it, so this goal is pushed to the 8 May 2001. Here it can be obtained from an uncut diamond, which itself can be obtained from mining.*
