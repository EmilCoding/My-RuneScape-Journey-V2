## Goals

### Skills

Since I am playing on RuneScape 3, I will adjust the level requirements to focus on activities instead of strict historical correctness. For example, I will need to get 40 Attack and Defence so that I can wield and use adamant weapons and armour.

- [x] 40 Attack - *To wield adamand weapons.*
- [x] 40 Defence - *To wield adamand armour.*
- [x] 40 Mining - *Required to mine adamantite.*
- [x] 40 Smithing - *Required to smith adamant*
- [x] 4 Prayer - *Burst of Strength*
- [x] 35 Magic - *Fire bolt*

Notes:
- Since PrayGood and PrayEvil had no unlocks, "Burst of Strength" was a Good Magic spell the player could cast [(source)](../../../day%20of%20release/skills.md#GoodMagic). To reflect this, the prayer requirement is 4, which is the current requirement to use the "Burst of Strength" prayer.
- Since "shock bolt" was the strongest spell on release [(source)](../../../day%20of%20release/skills.md#Magic), we have to cast something similar. Since its name includes "bolt" and uses fire runes, the best match is "fire bolt" in modern RuneScape. The spell requires 35 Magic to cast, so this is the requirement.
