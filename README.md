## What is it?

This repository documents the journey of my RuneScape 3 account throughout the ages of RuneScape. Starting from 4 January 2001, I play through every update and complete a set of goals that I have set myself. In this way, I experience every facet of the game as they were released. Goals can be quest-related, skill-related, monster-related, etc. When all challenges are completed I progress to the next update with new challenges. Using this method, I will experience RuneScape's history one snapshot at a time. It is my goal to get as far as possible before I get bored or real life prevents me from playing.

### Eras

I have split the game into different eras that define it. *This list is not completed yet.*

**Day of release**: 4 January 2001

The first day of the game. Not much content was available yet, but still many goals remained to be completed. Since I started on a new account, all my skills were minimal. I therefore had to raise many skills before I could continue, and I treat this single day as its own era because I spent a lot of time there.

**Free-to-play**: 4 January 2001 - 27 February 2002

The free-to-play era of RuneScape. It was still a small game but with a lot of content. A lot of quests were released here together with many skill-related updates. Since my skills started low, many skill updates took a long time because I had to raise my skills to complete the tasks.

**Member**: 27 February 2002 - 29 March 2004

Membership was released together with fishing. Many new quests, skills, and other content were added.

**Pre-split**: 29 March 2004 - 10 August 2007

RuneScape 2 was released with a lot of new content. The pre-split label refers to the split between the modern version of RuneScape and Old School RuneScape. The two games diverged on 10 August 2007. For more information see the [RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Old_School_RuneScape)


**Post-split**: 10 August 2007 - 20 November 2012

Still RuneScape 2, but after the Old School/Modern split. Iconic bosses and quests were released here, including the God Wars Dungeon, Corporeal Beast, Ritual of the Mahjarrat, etc. On 20 November 2012, the [Evolution of Combat](https://runescape.wiki/w/Evolution_of_Combat) was released, which changed the combat system completely. The start of this new era did not go well, as many players disliked the update and quit the game. This drove the community to private servers, which eventually led Jagex to poll the release of Old School RuneScape based on an old 10 August 2007 backup. This kickstarted OSRS, which became the more popular game of the two. [RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Old_School_RuneScape).

## My rules

### Strongest weapon

At release, the strongest melee weapon was the adamant two-handed sword. Therefore, I had to smith that weapon as a goal. That required training both Mining and Smithing to level 40 to collect and smith adamant bars. I also need the appropriate Attack level to wield it. Later, when Rune was released, I had to get a rune weapon, and when Dragon was released, I had to get a dragon weapon. I should always have access to the strongest weapon available at a given point in time.

### Boss release

At any point in time, I have to defeat the strongest monster available in the game. On the day-of-release, this was a level 46 black knight. Later, a level 62 moss giant was released, and so on, following the list of [strongest monsters](https://runescape.wiki/w/Strongest_monster).

When a boss is released, I must attempt to obtain its unique drops, aiming for at least 100 kills before continuing.

### My stance on minigames

Minigames are supposed to be fun, but many are not. They either require a lot of people to participate or are single-player minigames that take tens of hours to fully complete. That is not fun and does very little to progress your account. I will therefore skip them by default, though I still document them and add them to the backlog.

### Backlogging

Sometimes a goal is too absurd or impossible at a given moment. If so, I can backlog it. For example, on the day-of-release, the requirement for smithing an adamant platebody was 99, which was completely absurd. Shortly after release, the requirement was dropped first to 88 and later to 40. Therefore, the 99 smithing goal can be backlogged, since the methods available at the beginning of the game did not enable such a high skill. Details are further elaborated in the [backlog](./backlog.md) document.



## Tiers of completion

Naturally, you can complete an update to different extents. Therefore, I have developed a few tiers of completion. To get a given tier of completion, I have to satisfy a set of requirements for a given update and all the updates preceding it. I have made a list of tiers below.

| Tier | Explanation
| ---- | ------------
| Any% | Any of the tiers below
| [Skill%](#skill) | All skills must satisfy the minimum-skill-front
| [Quest%](#quest) | All quests and miniquests must be completed
| [MasterQuest%](#quest) | Satisfy the requirements of the Master Quest Cape.
| [Max%](#max) | You must reach the maximum level of all skills
| [Minigame%](#minigame) | Get all unique rewards from minigames
| [Collection%](#collection) | You must have filled all collection log slots, except treasure trails and legendary drops
| [Collection+%](#collection) | You must have filled all collection log slots including legendary drops, excluding treasure trails
| [Treasure%](#treasure) | You must have filled all collection log slots related to treasure trails, excluding legendary items
| [Treasure+%](#treasure) | You must have filled all collection log slots related to treasure trails, including legendary items
| [Completionist%](#completionist) | Satisfy all requirements for the Completionist Cape
| [Completionist+%](#completionist) | Satisfy all requirements for the Trimmed Completionist Cape
| Max% | All of the above tiers completed

### Skill%

For a given update, there is a set of minimum requirements that are needed to complete the update. The combination of all minimum skill requirements for a given update and all preceding updates forms the minimum-skill-front. The Skill% is awarded if my stats are equal to or higher than the minimum-skill-front.

### Quest%

I must complete all quests and mini-quests. If all post-quest-related content is also completed, then the **MasterQuest%** is achieved. This is covered by the [Master Quest Cape achievement](https://runescape.wiki/w/Master_Quest_Cape_(achievement)), with a few possible exceptions.

### Max%

For a given update, if I have reached the maximum level of every skill released at the time, I earn Max%. The maximum level here refers to the maximum level possible at that time. For example, on release, the Construction skill had a maximum level of 99. On 27 July 2026, its maximum level was raised to 120. To earn Max%, I only need to reach level 99 in Construction as long as my current date is earlier than 27 July 2026.

### Collection%

This tier is all about collecting rare items. That includes items in the Slayer and boss collection logs but also other rare items like the [satchels](https://runescape.wiki/w/Satchel) or the [monkey skull](https://runescape.wiki/w/Monkey_skull). **It does not include treasure trails items**.

There is one exception, though. Some items are extremely rare and have no good way to grind them. Therefore, some items are promoted to legendary drops. These include [shield left half](https://runescape.wiki/w/Shield_left_half), [draconic visage](https://runescape.wiki/w/Draconic_visage), [dragon full helm](https://runescape.wiki/w/Dragon_full_helm), etc. If a player also obtains them, they earn the upgraded tier of **Collection+%**.

### Treasure%

This tier works similarly to [Collection%](#collection), but focuses on clue scroll items. Here too, there are legendary drops such as the [gilded armour set](https://runescape.wiki/w/Gilded_armour) or [third age equipment](https://runescape.wiki/w/Third_age_melee_set). If all legendary drops are collected, the tier is upgraded to Treasure+%.

One could also add a subset of tiers for the different clue levels: TreasureEasy%, TreasureMedium%, TreasureHard%, TreasureElite%, TreasureMaster%, and TreasureGeneral%. That might come later.

### Minigame%

The Minigame% includes all rewards related to minigames, including the [Castle Wars capes](https://runescape.wiki/w/Castle_Wars_reward_capes). I don't think I will complete many of these, but it is still a tier. Read my opinion on minigames [here](#my-stance-on-minigames).


### Completionist%

This one includes all goals on the [Completionist Cape achievement](https://runescape.wiki/w/Completionist_Cape_(achievement)), but limits them to the requirements that existed on the release days of those achievements. That is, I can achieve Completionist% with 1 Divination as long as it is before 20 August 2013, the release day of Divination.

The same rules apply to the [Trimmed Completionist Cape achievement](https://runescape.wiki/w/Trimmed_Completionist_Cape_(achievement)), rewarding the player with **Completionist+%**.
