## Other achievments

### Quest Cape

The quest cape can only be optained when completing all the quest. I therefore use the "historial quest cape" achievment, which I made myself. That is completing all quest up to my current date.

### I've Got a Little List (of Miniquests)

Same system as for the "quest cape" achievment.

### Master Skiller

Requires reaching the maximum level of each skill. Again, I will use the "historical max cape"


### Kudos to You 

All kudos can be optained expect for 10 at release of completionist cape. That being
- 5 kudos for talking to Mr. Mordaut after completing the Ritual of the Mahjarrat.
- 5 kudos for defeating the Queen black dragon.


### Music Maestro

Unlock all music up to a given date. The the [music-checklist](../tables/music-tracks-checklist.md) as a reference.
