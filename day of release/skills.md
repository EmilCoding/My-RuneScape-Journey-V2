## Attack

Increases your accuracy in melee combat. At the beginning, there was no attack level requirement to wield any weapon. That meant that a completely fresh account could, in principle, wield the strongest weapon, an adamant 2-handed sword. That is, of course, if anyone had the ability to make one, which no one had on release. See the section on [Smithing](#smithing) for why. Since these requirements were added later, it makes sense to talk about melee weapons here.

The iconic tier system is:

| Metal  | Tier | RS3 tier |
|--------|------| -------- |
|Bronze  |1     | 1        |
|Iron    |1     | 10       |
|Steel   |5     | 20       |
|Black   |10    | 25       |
|Mithril |20    | 30       |
|Adamant |30    | 40       |

The iconic rune and dragon metals had not been released yet, so adamant was the most powerful.

## Defense

Decreases the damage taken when attacked. Like with attack, at the beginning of the game there were no level requirements for wearing anything. The same tier system for metal armour applies here as for weapons. See the section on [Attack](#attack).

## Strength

Increases your maximum hit in melee combat. Nothing more than that.

## Hits

Increases your maximum hitpoints. All players start at level 10.

**Fun fact**: At the beginning of the game, 1000 experience points were required for level 10. Therefore, all new accounts started with 1000 hitpoint XP. Since the XP formula was later changed, 1000 hitpoint XP only yielded level 9. This implies that a very small set of accounts, created at the beginning of the game and that had not trained their hitpoint levels, would have level 9 hitpoints, which is otherwise impossible.

## Ranged

Increases the accuracy and damage of ranged combat.

Earlier, when I said “arrows,” I mean the item formerly known as “arrow” in plural. There was only one type of arrow, now known as bronze arrow, and one kind of bolt, named crossbow bolt. These were the only kinds of ammunition.

## Magic

|Level| Name              | Description Runes           | required                              |
|-----|-------------------|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------|

### EvilMagic

| Level | Name           | Description Runes                       | required                                            |
|-------|----------------|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Confuse        | Reduces your opponent's fighting skills | 2 Mind-Runes, 1 Body-Rune                           |
| 1     | Thick skin     | Increases your defence (+5)             | 2 Earth-Runes, 2 Body-Runes                         |
| 4     | Shock bolt     | A strength 1 missile attack             | 2 Fire-Runes, 1 Air-Rune                            |

It was merged with GoodMagic and replaced with Magic soon after.

Runes were quite uncommon and therefore very expensive. They were primarily dropped by monsters or bought from stores. With limited store capacity and many players, buying from stores must have been difficult at times.

## Cooking

On release, fishing had not been released yet, so the only cookable items were:

- Bread - Made from water and flour.
- Cooked meat from various animals.

Neither of these healed much, but could easily be obtained.

## Woodcutting

Only one type of tree. Metal axes used the same tier metal system as the other skills.

## Firemaking

With only one type of log, there was only one thing you could do: burn regular logs. This did not change throughout the entire history of RuneScape Classic.

## Mining

Mining - The ability to dig precious metal from the ground. The ranking of the metals is the same as for [Attack](#attack) and [Defence](#defense), but the levels are definitely not the same.

For the metals, the level required to mine the different minerals was:

| Rock           | Ore            | Level |
|----------------|----------------|-------|
|Copper rock     | Copper ore     | 1     |
|Tin rock        | Tin ore        | 1     |
|Iron rock       | Iron ore       | 15    |
|Coal rock       | Coal           | 30    |
|Gold rock       | Gold           | 40    |
|Mithril rock    | Mithril ore    | 55    |
|Adamantite rock | Adamantite ore | 70    |

On the day of release, there was only a single type of pickaxe called "pickaxe".

## Smithing

|Level |Bar         |Ore                    |
|------|------------|-----------------------|
|1     |Bronze      | 1 Copper + 1 Tin      |
|15    |Iron        | 1 Iron*               |
|30    |Steel       | 1 Iron + 2 Coal       |
|40    |Gold        | 1 Gold                |
|55    |Mithril     | 1 Mithril + 4 Coal    |
|70    |Adamantite  | 1 Adamantite + 6 Coal |

*There is a 50% chance of not being successful in making iron. If it failed, the player would lose that ore.

## Tailoring

It was never developed and was removed on 23 June 2001.

## Carpentry

It was never developed. It was an early concept for Construction. There were even player-owned houses in Varrock, which were never opened for players before being removed.

## Influence

A proto-version of the Quest Point system was released later.
