# The world map

Close to RuneScape's release date, the world map looked like the image below. In this document, the locations are described.
![The RuneScape classic map](https://classic.runescape.wiki/images/Map-January-2001.gif?39dd7)

## Lumbridge

Ahhh, Lumbridge... The city where all new players start their adventures, at least on release. It is also the place you respawn if you die. It is part of the Misthalin kingdom and houses Duke Horacio of Lumbridge. He lives in the mighty Lumbridge Castle, an icon for the city. Next to the city lies the River Lum, which separates Lumbridge from the goblin camp. Crossing the bridge can be dangerous for the unprepared.

![Lumbridge](https://classic.runescape.wiki/images/thumb/Lumbridge.png/600px-Lumbridge.png?650c3)

### NPC

- Hans - The first NPC of RuneScape. He can tell you how old your account is.
- Cook - The player can start the "Cook's Assistant" quest by talking to him.
- Bob - Owner of Bob's Axes, a store that sells a pickaxe, an axe, and battleaxes. It is not clear whether Bob's Axes sold battleaxes on release. [Source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Bob%27s_Axes).
- Priest - The player can start "The Restless Ghost" quest by talking to him.
- Fred the Farmer - The player can start the "Sheep Shearer" quest by talking to him.

### Features

- Furnace
- Lumbridge's General Store
- Lumbridge's Church
- Bob's Axes
- Lumbridge Castle

### Monsters

- Rat - Level 2
- Goblin - Level 7
- Man - Level 9

## Lumbridge swamp

Going south of Lumbridge, you enter Lumbridge Swamp. Here, coal, mithril, and adamantite rocks can be found alongside Father Urhney's hut and many level-8 rats.

![East side](https://classic.runescape.wiki/images/Lumbridge_swamp_%28east%29.png?d4515)
![West side](https://classic.runescape.wiki/images/Lumbridge_swamp_%28west%29.png?c4dce)

### Monsters in the swamp

- Level 8 rats

### Features in the swamp

- Coal rocks.
- Mithril rocks.
- Adamantite rocks.
- Many trees.

## Wizard's tower

Going west of the swamp, you arrive at the Wizard's Tower. On the day of release, no wizards were actually present in the tower [(source)](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Wizard%27s_Tower#Trivia). Its only purpose is in "The Restless Ghost" quest, where the player needs to obtain a skull.
![Getting the skull in Restless Ghost.](https://classic.runescape.wiki/images/Restless_ghost_skull.png?0b8b1)

## Draynor Manor

Moving north of the Wizard's Tower, the mighty Draynor Manor appears before you. The manor cannot be entered, but beware: there are undead trees that attack players. [Source](https://runescape.wiki/w/Update:Newsletter_1)

![Draynor manor](https://classic.runescape.wiki/images/Draynor_Manor.png?46345)

## Ghost Town

At the top of the River Lum lies a ghost town with no people—empty streets and houses. It has a dungeon, though, filled with monsters. The dungeon can be entered from the other end. There is a ladder in a little shed, which requires the brass key to unlock. The key can be found in the dungeon, which requires you to enter the ghost town at least once.

### The mine

- Copper
- Tin
- Iron
- Coal
- Mithril
- Adamantite
