# Equipment

To determine what armour is best, it is first necessary to understand what parameters we are working with. There are five different parameters:

- Weapon aim - *Reduces the probability of hitting zero for melee attacks.*
- Weapon Power - *Increases the maximum hit of melee attacks.*
- Armour - *Reduces the chance of being hit.*
- Magic - *It is believed that the magic bonus decreases the chance of failure when casting spells. ([source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Magic#Equipment))*
- Prayer - *Has currently no function, since the prayer skill has no function and no items provide this bonus.*

## Weapon stats explained

### Weapon aim

[Adamant 2-handed sword](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Adamantite_2-handed_Sword) has the highest weapon aim of 44, but requires two hands to use. The highest value for one-handed weapons is 31 and belongs to the [adamant long sword](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Adamantite_Long_Sword).

### Weapon Power

[Adamant 2-handed sword](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Adamantite_2-handed_Sword) has the highest weapon power of 44, but requires two hands to use. The highest value for one-handed weapons is 41 and belongs to the [adamant battle axe](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Adamantite_battle_Axe).

*While wearing an adamant plate body, you cannot use any gloves. The chainmail body can be used together with gloves. Except for a few cases, the gloves do not provide enough bonus to outweigh the use of plate bodies.

### Magic bonus

It is believed that the magic bonus decreases the chance of failure when casting spells. ([source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Magic#Equipment))

Only two non-weapon items provide this bonus:

- Wizard hat (Black) (+3) - [source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Wizardshat_(blue))
- Wizard hat (Blue) (+4) - [source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Wizardshat_(blue))

There were three types of staves:

- Staff (+6) - [source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Staff)
- Magic staff (+15) - [source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Magic_Staff)
- Plain elemental staves (+20) - [source](https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/Elemental_staff)

All elemental staves have the same magic bonus and provide an unlimited supply of their corresponding rune. The best melee bonuses come from the staff of fire.

### Prayer bonus

No equipment gives any prayer bonuses yet.

### Ranged

There are only four ranged weapons:

- Shortbow - Can shoot arrows.
- Longbow - Can shoot arrows, but has a longer range than the shortbow.
- Crossbow - Can shoot crossbow bolts.
- Phoenix crossbow - Same as a crossbow.
There is only one kind of arrow and only a single kind of bolt.
